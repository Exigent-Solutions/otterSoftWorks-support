## Asking for help

1. Check the [install guide](https://www.ottersoftworks.com/install/), which covers the most common problems.
2. Search the [existing issues](../../issues?q=is%3Aissue) in case someone has asked already.
3. Otherwise, [open a new issue](../../issues/new/choose) and pick **Report a problem** or **Suggest an idea**.

You need a free GitHub account to open an issue.

## Everything here is public

Our apps are built to keep your information to yourself. Everything they store stays on your
phone: they have no permission to use the internet and never send your data to us or anyone else.
We'll never ask you for it.

Anyone can read issues, and search engines can find them, so please leave out:

- names, phone numbers, email and home addresses, and account details, yours or anyone else's
- the text of your messages, notes or time entries. Describe what happened instead; for a spam
  text that got through, or a real one that was blocked, retype it with names, numbers and links changed
- exported files and backups

Crop or blur screenshots so no names, numbers or message text show. If we need more detail, we'll
ask you to describe it or try it with made-up data.

To report a security problem, don't open an issue. See [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).
